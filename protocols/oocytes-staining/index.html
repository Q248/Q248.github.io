Oocytes Immunostaining

Adapted from So et al., 2019.

Buffers

Buffer Composition
Fixative 100 mM HEPES (pH 7.0, titrated with KOH), 50 mM EGTA (pH 7.0, titrated with KOH), 10 mM MgSO₄, 2% PFA (methanol-free) and 0.5% Triton X-100
Washing buffer 0.3% Triton X-100 in PBS
Blocking buffer 0.3% Triton X-100, 3% BSA and 5% NDS in PBS

Procedure

From step 5 onward, all incubations and washes are performed with gentle rocking.

  1. Dissect the ovaries in M2 medium containing 2.5 µM milrinone.
  2. Puncture antral follicles with a syringe needle to release fully grown GV oocytes; release growing oocytes from secondary follicles in the same way.
  3. Remove the surrounding granulosa cells by gently pipetting the oocytes through a glass capillary with an opening slightly smaller than the oocyte.
  4. Fix in fixative for 15 min at 37 °C.
  5. Wash three times in washing buffer, 10 min each, at room temperature.
  6. Block in blocking buffer for 1 h at room temperature.
  7. Incubate with primary antibodies in blocking buffer overnight at 4 °C.
  8. Wash three times in blocking buffer, 10 min each, at room temperature.
  9. Incubate with secondary antibodies in blocking buffer for 1 h at room temperature.
  10. Wash three times in washing buffer, 5 min each, at room temperature; add DAPI (1 µg/mL) during the second wash.
  11. Mount on a slide in SlowFade mountant and image.

Notes

  • Before fixation, oocytes can be kept in M16 medium containing 2.5 µM milrinone at 37 °C (5% CO₂).
