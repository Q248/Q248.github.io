## Procedure

1. Fix the tissue in 10% NBF at room temperature for 48 h.
2. Dehydrate through a graded ethanol series (diluted in ddH₂O): 50% ethanol for 30 min; 70% ethanol, 2 × 1 h; 95% ethanol, 2 × 1 h; 100% ethanol overnight.
3. Clear in xylene, 2 × 1.5 h; then infiltrate with xylene:paraffin (1:1) at 65 °C for 1 h, followed by paraffin at 65 °C overnight.
4. Embed the tissue in paraffin.
5. Cut 5 µm sections and dry the slides overnight on a slide warmer.
6. Deparaffinize and rehydrate: xylene, 3 × 3 min; 100% ethanol, 3 × 3 min; 95% ethanol, 3 min; 80% ethanol, 3 min; dH₂O, 5 min.
7. Stain with hematoxylin: hematoxylin, 3 min; tap water, 5 min; acid ethanol, 3–5 quick dips; tap water, 2 × 1 min; dH₂O, 2 min.
8. Stain with eosin and dehydrate: eosin, 30 s; 95% ethanol, 2 × 5 min; 100% ethanol, 2 × 5 min; xylene, 2 × 15 min.
9. Mount with Micromount, apply a coverslip, and let the slides dry before imaging.

## Notes

- Perform all steps involving formalin or xylene in a fume hood.
