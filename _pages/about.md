I am a developmental biologist investigating the cellular and molecular mechanisms that govern **oocyte development and follicle dynamics**, and how these processes shape female fertility.

In [Dr. Allan Spradling's laboratory](https://carnegiescience.edu/), I identified distinct functional waves of ovarian follicles in the mouse and showed that wave 1 follicles primarily promote theca cell formation and androgen production rather than directly supporting fertility.

My current research focuses on **nuclear positioning** and **acentriolar microtubule-organizing center (acMTOC) dynamics** during oocyte growth and meiotic preparation.

Before joining Carnegie, I was a research associate at the Gurdon Institute, University of Cambridge, and received my Ph.D. in Developmental Biology from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, where I worked on haploid embryonic stem cells and CRISPR-based genetic screening in mice.
