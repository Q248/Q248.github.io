Reagents and Materials

Item Supplier Catalog no.
PBS Thermo Fisher 10010-023

Triton X-100 MilliporeSigma X100-500ML
1-Thioglycerol MilliporeSigma M1753
Bovine serum albumin (BSA) MilliporeSigma A9647-50G
Normal donkey serum (NDS) Jackson ImmunoResearch 017-000-121

Histodenz MilliporeSigma D2158
DAPI MilliporeSigma D9542-10MG
Glass-bottom dish MatTek P35G-0-14-C

Buffers

Buffer Composition
Fixative 4% PFA in PBS
Washing buffer 0.3% Triton X-100 and 0.5% 1-thioglycerol in PBS
Blocking buffer 0.3% Triton X-100, 1% BSA and 1% NDS in PBS
Dilution buffer 0.3% Triton X-100 and 1% BSA in PBS
Clearing buffer 4 g Histodenz dissolved in 2.75 mL of 40% N-methylacetamide in PBS, with 25 µL 1-thioglycerol (0.5%) and 10 µL Triton X-100 (0.2%)

Procedure

All incubations are performed with gentle rocking.

  1. Dissect the ovaries in PBS.
  2. Fix in 4% PFA overnight at 4 °C.
  3. Wash three times in washing buffer, 30 min each, at room temperature.
  4. Block in blocking buffer overnight at 37 °C.
  5. Incubate with primary antibodies in dilution buffer for 2 days at 37 °C.
  6. Wash in washing buffer overnight at 37 °C.
  7. Incubate with secondary antibodies in dilution buffer for 2 days at 37 °C.
  8. Wash in washing buffer overnight at 37 °C, then for 1 more day at room temperature with DAPI (1 µg/mL).
  9. Clear in clearing buffer for 2 days at room temperature.
  10. Mount in clearing buffer in a glass-bottom dish and image.

Notes

  • Histodenz dissolves slowly; prepare the clearing buffer in advance (e.g., overnight with rotation).
  • Incubation times may need to be adjusted for ovary size and age; larger (adult) ovaries may need longer incubations.
  • Cleared samples can be stored in clearing buffer at 4 °C in the dark until imaging, or at −20 °C for long-term preservation.

Reference
